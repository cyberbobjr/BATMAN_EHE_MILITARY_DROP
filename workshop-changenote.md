## 0.8.1 — 2026-10-29

### Easier to find military documents

- Military memos are now five times more likely on eligible military and police zombies: 1 in 10 by default. Codebooks are five times more likely on military zombies: 1 in 20 by default.
- Search police desks, filing cabinets, file boxes, evidence shelves and armouries for both documents. Military storage, bunker lockers, barracks lockers, and army surplus literature shelves can also hold both.
- Police and fire station lockers can now hold military memos.
- Your existing rarity selections are kept, with increased chances. The notes and codebook settings also control their frequency in the new storage locations.
- Existing worlds are supported: storage additions apply to containers filled after the update. Already generated loot is not refilled.

With the default encrypted code, you still need a memo for the frequencies, a codebook and the shortwave numbers station. Documents remain random finds rather than guaranteed rewards for clearing a building.
