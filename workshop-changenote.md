## 0.8.0 — 2026-10-27

**KEEP THE LINE OPEN**

Stay connected to a base that needs you: restore damaged relays, answer changing supply requests and earn recognition for your service.

### Restore communications

Storms and power-grid failures can interrupt your supply requests. Find the damaged relay on your map, bring electronic scrap and head out to repair it. Your Electricity skills will make a difference.

### See your missions through

A cleared area deserves a clear confirmation! A personal banner announces your success, a green checkmark briefly marks the secured area and the liaison post keeps a history of your results.

Recon objectives and damaged relays also receive map markers to guide you to your next assignment.

### Answer the base's needs

Fulton cargo priorities now change every week. Ask what the command needs and adapt your shipments to earn extra recognition.

You can also report concentrations of infected and confirm that your supply drops arrived safely.

### Earn recognition for your service

Your loyalty does not go unnoticed. As you reach new trust tiers, personal commendations arrive with your next eligible supply drop.

### Jam the airwaves in multiplayer

The new **optional RJ-93 submod** adds a radio jammer you can craft and deploy in the field. Powered by a battery, it disrupts nearby Military Drop requests — including your own.

Use its control window to monitor the battery, insert or remove it and choose when to transmit. Static and an explicit link status warn you when communications are jammed. Public broadcasts remain audible, and the main mod works without this submod.

### Recover, dismantle, reuse

Flight recorders now remain in your possession after transmission. Identify those already sent to the base and dismantle them to recover useful materials.

### Administration made easier

A new console brings together tools for managing drops, missions, relays, survivors and wrecks. Available to multiplayer admins and in singleplayer debug mode, it lets you check communications, launch missions and intervene through clearly explained actions.

### Also in this update

- Separate controls for helicopter noise and delivery noise; the configured drop horde still spawns.
- Relay orders and repair instructions in the liaison post.
- Improved recon marker synchronization after reconnecting.
- Clearer mission details and identification of already-transmitted recorders.
- Interface fixes, including access to the solo Debug menu.
