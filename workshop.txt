description=[h2]When a helicopter goes down[/h2]
description=
description=A supply helicopter can crash, more often in storms. Its MAYDAY gives a sector on the military frequency. At the wreck, the pilot carries a memo, a codebook and the [b]flight recorder[/b]; strip the wreck for parts, then cut it up. Supplies still land at the site.

description=[h2]Contested drop zones (PvP)[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/steam/zones-add.png[/img]
description=
description=On PvP servers, the admin picks where crates fall: zones grouped into sectors (a park, a mall, flats in Louisville). Each drop takes the nearest or a random sector, then a zone by weight: nobody can camp the spot. The announcement names it ("LZ Central Park, grid..."); players can optionally choose the sector in the form, decoy included. The crate never lands in water, inside a building, in a non-PvP zone or a safehouse.
description=
description=Admin panel in game ([b]Military Drop Zones[/b]): draw zones with the mouse, set sector and weight, [b]Highlight[/b] them on the ground, edit, disable, teleport. Off by default.
description=
description=[h2]For server admins[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]60 sandbox options, applied without restarting (except frequencies): wait between drops (one week by default, for the whole server), distance, horde, code mode, trust, missions, requisition, decoy, crashes, drop zones.
description=[*]Requisition lots can be edited, disabled or added in Zomboid/Lua/MilitaryDrop/requisition.txt.
description=[*]Server-authoritative multiplayer. Admins can force a drop, crash the next helicopter and launch missions from a military radio.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

description=[list]
