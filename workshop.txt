description=Tune a military radio to the right frequency, give the authentication code, and request an airdrop of military surplus. A helicopter flies in and drops a supply crate... and the noise draws the dead.
description=
description=[h2]Planned features[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Call a supply drop with a military walkie-talkie or HAM radio.
description=[*]The frequency is found on notes carried by military and police zombies. The authentication code changes every in-game week: a numbers station sends it encrypted, and military codebooks decipher it (or, in the sandbox options, a fixed or weekly code written in clear on the notes).
description=[*]A helicopter you can hear and see approach: moving sound, rotor shadow on the ground, direction marker.
description=[*]Crate loot comes from the game's army distribution tables, so it works with weapon mods.
description=[*]Anyone listening on the frequency hears the drop coordinates, and the spot is marked on their map.
description=[*]Earn the base's trust as a faction: daily reports, soldiers' dog tags, public missions and a command post radio.
description=[*]Server-authoritative multiplayer, with one server-wide cooldown set in the sandbox options.
description=[*]Standalone: no other mod required. Compatible with HEF - Helicopter Event Framework. With Signal Smoke, green smoke marks the crate.
description=[/list]
