Earlier drops do not affect the new scores. Mission progress and daily limits become personal. Shared posts and call signs remain.

description=
description=Drop changes apply only to the requesting character; a different opener gets 0. Other rewards go to the acting character. Times are game time; values are defaults.

description=Daily cap: +8 per character, excluding drops. Liaison post bonus: report +2, tag +3, recon +5, radio check +2; clearance stays +5. Scores: 0–100; a loss below 15 cuts the line for 3 days. Expired missions, admin drops and decoys: 0. Erosion is off by default and moves the score toward 25.

description=
description=[list]
description=[*]45 sandbox options: wait between drops (one week by default, for the whole server), distance, horde, code mode, trust, missions, requisition, decoy.
description=[*]Requisition lots can be edited, disabled or added in Zomboid/Lua/MilitaryDrop/requisition.txt.
description=[*]Server-authoritative multiplayer. Admins can force a drop and launch missions from a military radio.
