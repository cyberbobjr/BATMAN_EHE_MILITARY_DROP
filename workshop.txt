description=[b]Build 42.21 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer · Standalone[/b]
description=
description=[i]Multiplayer: the mod is built for dedicated servers (the server decides everything), but it has only been tested in singleplayer so far. Please report any multiplayer issue.[/i]
description=
description=Tune a military radio to the right frequency, give this week's code and requisition military supplies. A helicopter drops a crate far away and announces the grid to everyone listening... and the noise draws the dead.
description=
description=[h2]Find the frequency and the code[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/steam-code.png[/img]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/ingame-codebook-memo.png[/img]
description=
description=Military and police zombies carry memos with the military frequency, different in every game. The code changes every Monday: a numbers station broadcasts it on shortwave, and a military codebook deciphers it.
description=
description=[h2]Call from your radio[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/steam-radio.png[/img]
description=

description=
description=Right-click a military radio, [b]Device Options[/b], [b]Logistics[/b] section: type the code and press [b]Request a supply drop[/b]. Walkie-talkie in hand, on the belt or in a bag, or a placed radio.
description=
description=[h2]Fill the requisition[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/steam-requisition.png[/img]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/requisition-form-en.png[/img]
description=
description=Spend a point budget on 18 lots drawn from the game's own loot tables, mods included: rations, medical, ammunition, firearms, fuel... Or order a siren decoy that drags the hordes to a sector you choose.
description=
description=[h2]Reach the crate[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/steam-crate.png[/img]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/ingame-crate-trunk.png[/img]
description=
description=Hear the helicopter, see its shadow, then race to the announced grid, 150 to 400 tiles away. Everyone on the frequency gets the map marker, and a horde guards the crate. Empty crates can be dismantled for wood.
description=
description=[h2]Earn the base's trust[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/steam-trust.png[/img]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/ingame-console-missions.png[/img]
description=
description=Recover your drops, send daily reports, announce fallen soldiers' dog tags and answer public missions: recon, horde clearance, radio checks. Trust shortens the wait and unlocks bigger requisitions.
description=
description=[h2]Hold a liaison post[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/steam-post.png[/img]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/guide/images/console-en.png[/img]
description=
description=Turn a fixed military ham radio into your station's console: transmission log, orders, dog tags, standing and drop requests. In multiplayer, each faction is a station with its own call sign.
description=
description=[h2]For server admins[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]44 sandbox options: wait between drops (one week by default, for the whole server), distance, horde, code mode, trust, missions, requisition, decoy.
description=[*]Requisition lots can be edited, disabled or added in Zomboid/Lua/MilitaryDrop/requisition.txt.
description=[*]Server-authoritative multiplayer. Admins can force a drop and launch missions from a military radio.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]No other mod required.
description=[*]Designed to coexist with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3672792485]HEF - Helicopter Event Framework[/url].
description=[*]With [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811010882]Signal Smoke[/url], green smoke marks the crate.
description=[*]Items from your weapon and item mods appear in the crates.
description=[/list]
