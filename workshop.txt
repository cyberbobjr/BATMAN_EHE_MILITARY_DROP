description=Tune a military radio to the right frequency, give this week's code and requisition supplies. A helicopter drops a crate far away and announces the grid to everyone listening... and the noise draws the dead.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/main/docs/images/screenshots/walkie-logistics.png[/img]
description=
description=[olist]
description=[*][b]Find the frequency and the code.[/b] Military and police memos give the frequency; every Monday, decipher the shortwave numbers station with the military codebook.
description=[*][b]Call from a military radio.[/b] Right-click it, [b]Device Options[/b], [b]Logistics[/b] section: type the code and press [b]Request a supply drop[/b].
description=[*][b]Fill the requisition form.[/b] 18 lots built from the loot tables (your mods included), within a budget set by your trust. Or a siren decoy to lure hordes away.
description=[*][b]Reach the crate.[/b] It lands 150 to 400 tiles away, guarded by a horde. Keep a radio on the military frequency: the grid is repeated until a case is opened.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]New: Fulton extraction[/h2]
description=
description=Send intelligence to the army by balloon. Request a pick-up with [b]Request a Fulton pick-up[/b], put a [b]Fulton recovery kit[/b] on the ground outside, fill it with ID cards, paperwork, stash maps or NBC gear, then [b]Inflate and release the Fulton[/b] with a [b]Helium tank[/b]. An aircraft snatches it and the base pays you in trust. Craft the kit or repair a damaged one found in army storage, wrecks or crates.
description=
description=[h2]Earn the base's trust[/h2]
description=
description=Each character has a personal reputation, never shown as a number. It shortens the wait between drops and unlocks bigger requisitions.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Open your own drop first, send a flight recorder from a wreck.
description=[*]Daily reports, dog tags of fallen soldiers, recon, clearance orders, radio checks, Fulton shipments.
description=[*]Lose it when outsiders open your drops or when a drop is lost.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]And also[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Helicopter crashes[/b], more frequent in storms: a MAYDAY gives the sector; the wreck holds parts, documents and a flight recorder.
description=[*][b]Liaison post[/b]: a fixed military radio becomes your station's console (tasks, orders, log, recorder bay).
description=[*][b]Contested drop zones[/b] for PvP servers, drawn in game by the admin.
description=[*]71 sandbox options; server-authoritative multiplayer.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]0.8.0[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Clearance success notifications, result history at the liaison post and temporary green map checks. Recon and relay repair objectives are marked on your map.
description=[*]Repair relay cabinets after outages using electronic scrap; Electricity reduces cost and time. Earn XP, no generator required.
description=[*]Weekly Fulton cargo bonuses, horde reports, delivery receipts and personal commendations.
description=[*]Separate rotor/delivery noise controls; the configured drop horde remains.
description=[*]Admin console: SP Debug → Main; MP right-click anywhere → Military Drop Admin Menu. Current code/frequencies, forced drops, next crash and Fulton tests. Drop-zone menus unchanged.
description=[*]Optional [b]batman_MilitaryDropJammer[/b] submod in this package: craft and place the RJ-93, insert/remove a battery and view charge in its window. Default: 100 tiles, 24 game hours per full battery. Blocks outgoing Military Drop calls, including yours; broadcasts remain audible. Static, a message and link status identify jamming. Leave this submod disabled to play without it.
description=[*]Flight recorders: dismantle before or after transmission; the physical recorder is now returned. Admins can launch relay repair orders at a selected site.
description=[/list]
