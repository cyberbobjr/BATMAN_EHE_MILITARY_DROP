# FAQ

[Français](../fr/07-faq.md) · [Guide home](README.md) · Previous: [Server admin](06-server-admin.md)

**Nobody answers, only static.**
The frequency or the code is wrong, and the base does not say which. Check the frequency circled on a memo, decode this week's code again (it changes every Monday at 00:00), and make sure you use a military radio. After 3 wrong codes in a day, the base stays silent until the next day.

**I can't find any memo.**
Memos are carried by military and police zombies, about 1 in 50 by default. Look for checkpoints, police stations and army sites. The server admin can raise the rate or choose a code written in clear.

**The memo gives no code.**
With the default settings, the code is broadcast in code by the numbers station written on the memo, and deciphered with a codebook. See [Calling a drop](02-calling-a-drop.md).

**How long between two drops?**
One week of game time by default, for the whole server, shorter or longer depending on your trust. The base tells you how many hours are left.

**My radio is greyed, or the Logistics section is missing.**
Only military radios have the section (US Army Walkie Talkie, Manpack Radio, Ham Radio). It must be on. A placed US Army Ham Radio shows only the **Liaison post** button.

**I missed the announcement. Where is the crate?**
The marker appears only on maps of players listening at that moment. Ask someone who was listening, or read the log of your [liaison post](05-liaison-post.md) if it was on.

**I reached the grid but there is no crate.**
The crate is placed when someone arrives and the area is loaded, up to 30 tiles from the announced point. Look around.

**My form closed / expired.**
The form is valid for 5 real minutes. Nothing was spent: call again.

**Can other players steal my drop?**
Yes. Everyone tuned to the military frequency hears the grid. If another station opens your crate first, your station loses 5 trust.

**Do the other players know it is a decoy?**
No. A decoy looks exactly like a real drop until someone opens the trunk.

**Does it work with weapon and item mods?**
Yes. Crates draw from the game's loot tables, including your mods' items.

**Do I need Expanded Helicopter Events?**
No. Military Drop is standalone. HEF and Signal Smoke are optional companions.

**Where do I report a bug?**
On the Steam Workshop page or in the [GitHub issues](https://github.com/cyberbobjr/BATMAN_EHE_MILITARY_DROP/issues). Please attach `Zomboid/console.txt`, and enable the **Debug log** sandbox option if you can.
